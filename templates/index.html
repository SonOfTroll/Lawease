What this can’t do

Read this before you rely on anything above.

It is not your lawyer

Nothing here is legal advice and no advocate–client relationship exists. For anything that matters, take the output to someone admitted to practise.

The case model performs at chance

The outcome model scores 62.6% on held-out cases. Always guessing “petitioner wins” scores 64.8%, so the model beats nothing. Read the nearest decided cases it surfaces; ignore the percentage.

The record is American

Precedents come from 3,303 US Supreme Court cases. They are not authority in an Indian court, and the area-of-law labels follow US categories.

A machine read the contract

Contract X-Ray runs on a language model. It misses things, and it can be confidently wrong. Treat every flag as a question to ask, not a finding.
